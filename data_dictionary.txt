TerraSentinel-Nepal — Data Dictionary
======================================
Every column in melamchi_features.csv / melamchi_features.parquet.
Columns marked [PENDING] are populated once the corresponding phase runs.
Columns are NEVER filled with fabricated values — if a feature could not be
reliably produced, it is removed from the table and the reason is logged in
metadata.csv rather than left in here as a placeholder.

IDENTIFIERS
-----------
cell_id                        Unique ID of the 100m x 100m grid cell (grid/build_grid.py)
event_id                       One of MELAMCHI_2021, MELAMCHI_2026, RASUWA_2026 — never mixed
date                           Date associated with this row's observation (event-specific)
latitude, longitude            Centroid of the grid cell, WGS84 (EPSG:4326), decimal degrees

RAINFALL (NASA GPM IMERG) [PENDING]
------------------------------------
rainfall_1h ... rainfall_7day  Cumulative rainfall (mm) over the stated window ending at `date`,
                                from IMERG at native ~0.1 deg (~10km) resolution, resampled to the
                                grid — see metadata.csv for exact original resolution, do not assume
                                the grid resolution equals IMERG's native resolution.

TERRAIN (NASADEM/SRTM ~30m) [PENDING]
----------------------------------------
elevation                      Meters above sea level
slope                          Degrees
aspect                         Degrees from north (0-360), slope-facing direction
curvature                      Profile curvature (unitless, DEM-derivative)
flow_accumulation              Upslope contributing cells (unitless, D8 accumulation count)
distance_to_river               Meters to nearest mapped river channel
river_gradient                 Local channel slope (m/m) at nearest river reach

SENTINEL-2 / WATER [PENDING]
-------------------------------
ndwi_mean                      Mean NDWI = (Green[B03] - NIR[B08]) / (Green[B03] + NIR[B08]) for the cell, unitless [-1,1]
ndwi_change                    Post-event ndwi_mean minus pre-event ndwi_mean
water_area                     Water-classified area within the cell, m^2 (NDWI threshold-based mask)
water_area_change_pct          % change in water_area, pre- vs post-event

SENTINEL-1 SAR [PENDING]
---------------------------
sar_change                     Log-ratio (dB) change in backscatter, post vs pre event (VV, or VV+VH combined — documented per-run in metadata.csv)
displacement                   [May be dropped — see metadata.csv] InSAR-derived displacement, cm, if reliably obtainable
displacement_change             [May be dropped] Change in displacement between periods, cm

HISTORICAL HAZARDS [PENDING]
--------------------------------
historical_landslide_density   Landslide polygons per km^2 within a fixed radius of the cell centroid
distance_to_historical_landslide  Meters to nearest mapped historical landslide
distance_to_landslide_dam      Meters to nearest known landslide-dam location

GLACIAL LAKES (ICIMOD) [PENDING]
------------------------------------
distance_to_glacial_lake       Meters to nearest inventoried glacial lake
glacial_lake_area              Area (m^2) of that nearest lake
glacial_lake_area_change       % or absolute change in that lake's area, if historical imagery allows

TARGET
------
landslide_label                 0 = no known landslide, 1 = landslide, from historical hazard layer
